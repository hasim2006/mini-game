Frequently Asked Questions

Are all games on Mini Game Arcade completely free?

Yes, 100%! All games on Mini Game Arcade are completely free to play directly in your web browser. There are no paid microtransactions or subscriptions.

Do I need to install an app or download anything?

No downloads are required. Our games run entirely on modern web standards (HTML5 Canvas and Web Audio) and launch instantly on any compatible device.

How are my high scores saved?

High scores and audio preferences are saved locally on your device using HTML5 localStorage. They persist across sessions on the same device and browser.

Can I play on my mobile phone?

Yes! All games are built with responsive touch controls and fluid CSS layouts specifically tested for iOS and Android mobile browsers.
