Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At Mini Game Arcade (accessible from our website), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Mini Game Arcade and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

Mini Game Arcade is designed as a lightweight, client-side web gaming portal. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identity details to play our games.

However, standard automated technical information may be collected through standard server logs and third-party advertising partners as detailed below:

  • Log Files: Like most standard web servers, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records routine log data when you visit our website. This includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These logs are used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, preventing malicious traffic, and gathering aggregate demographic information. Log files are not linked to any personally identifiable information.
  • Local Storage (HTML5 localStorage): To enhance your gaming experience, we store non-personal gameplay preferences—specifically your high scores (e.g., fruit_slice_highscore) and audio mute preferences (arcade_muted)—locally within your own browser. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Mini Game Arcade uses standard cookies to store information about visitors' preferences and the specific pages that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and device configuration.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or Your Online Choices.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some of our advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Mini Game Arcade, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Note that Mini Game Arcade has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data (We do not sell personal information).

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users are entitled to the following rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of any personal data held.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to object: You have the right to object to our processing of your data.

7. Children's Information (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance. Mini Game Arcade does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will perform our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to reach out to us through our Contact Page.
