About Fruit Blade

Fruit Blade is an arcade reaction game built from scratch using the HTML5 Canvas API and Web Audio API. Fruits are thrown upwards in realistic physical parabolic arcs. Your objective is to slice as many fruits as possible with your blade without letting three fruits drop off screen or slicing explosive bombs.

Scoring & Multipliers

  • Standard Fruits: Watermelon, Orange, and Apple yield 10 base points each.
  • Bonus Fruits: Bananas and Strawberries yield 15 points each.
  • Combo Bonus: Slicing 2 or more fruits in a single fluid gesture awards an escalating point multiplier (+20 for 2x, +30 for 3x, etc.).
  • Strikes: You have 3 strikes. Missing an un-sliced fruit or slicing a bomb adds a strike. At 3 strikes, the game concludes.

Game Controls

  • Mouse / Drag Left Click + Swipe
  • Touch / Mobile Finger Swipe
  • Pause / Resume P or Spacebar
  • Toggle Sound M
